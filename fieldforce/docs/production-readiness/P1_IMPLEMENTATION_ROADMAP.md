# P1 Implementation Roadmap

P1 starts only after the P0 production gate is passed.

## Wave 1 — Workforce Control
- **P1.1 Dispatch & Eligibility:** geography, capability, certification, availability, workload, restrictions, risk.
- **P1.2 Availability & Capacity:** availability states, reserve targets, State/LGA/service capacity.

## Wave 2 — Workforce Quality
- **P1.3 Matching & Ranking:** explainable ranking using proximity, reliability, experience, QC and workload.
- **P1.4 Trust, Reputation & Tiers:** multidimensional, explainable reputation.
- **P1.5 Academy & Certification:** training, certification, expiry and operational eligibility.

## Wave 3 — Workforce Supply
- **P1.6 Recruitment:** candidate funnel and geographic/capability gap campaigns.
- **P1.7 Partner Network:** partner portal, candidate submissions and scoped analytics.

## Wave 4 — Commercial Scale
- **P1.8 Multi-Client Platform:** tenant isolation, service catalog, pricing/SLA, request-to-billing.

## Wave 5 — Operational Command
- **P1.9 Command Center:** mission board, map, agent pool, capacity, escalations, QC, finance exceptions.
- **P1.10 Safety & Escalation:** safety briefing, unsafe-location, pause, incidents, emergency escalation.
- **P1.11 Advanced QC:** service-specific rules, automated checks, client acceptance criteria and rework economics.

## Wave 6 — Intelligence
- **P1.12 AI Advisory:** summaries, recommendations, anomaly flags and forecasting assistance; never authoritative for mission, finance, trust or safety decisions.

## P1 success metrics
- Qualified-fill rate
- Time-to-assign / time-to-accept
- SLA completion
- First-pass QC / rework
- Capacity coverage
- Agent retention/progression
- Recruitment conversion
- Client repeat rate and gross margin
- Safety incident resolution
- AI recommendation acceptance vs override
